Referências visuais

HTMLs originais do export do Claude Design, preservados para consulta. Abrem direto no navegador (file://) e usam os tokens vivos de design-system/styles.css. Os cards de componente e o UI kit usam o bundle compilado do export em _runtime/ + React/Babel/Lucide via CDN (precisam de internet). Não são a implementação de produção — essa está em design-system/components.

Guidelines — fundamentos

  • Logoassets/logo-dark.svg · logo-light.svg · mark.svg
  • MotifsGhost numerals, gold rule, section counter, tracked footer
  • MotionSlow reveals, ease-out(.22,1,.36,1); no bounce
  • GoldPrimary accent — from the GLDN mark (#F0D98C · #D4AF37 · #9C7A1E)
  • Gradients & glowsMetallic gold for rules/numerals; ember glow for backgrounds
  • Ink neutralsWarm near-blacks → pearl
  • Colour ratio60 ink · 25 charcoal/glass · 10 gold · 5 pearl
  • Semantic surfaces & textAliases — use these in UI, not raw scales
  • StatusSparingly — dots, validation, badges
  • Elevation & glassshadow-card, shadow-float, glow-gold over ember glow
  • RadiiSoft, never bubbly — pills for buttons & eyebrows
  • Spacing scale4px base; sections breathe at 80–160px
  • Body — MontserratLight 300 for paragraphs; 500–600 for UI
  • Display — CinzelHeadlines, numerals, brand highlights. Bold 700.
  • Labels & eyebrowsMontserrat 500, uppercase, tracking .28–.42em
  • Type scaleDisplay 68 → caption 12.5
  • Two-tone headlineSignature: pearl statement + gold payoff line
